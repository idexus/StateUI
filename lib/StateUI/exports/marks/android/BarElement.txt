NavigationStack.barBackgroundColor: ✅
NavigationStack.barButtonBackgroundColor: – A toolbar's action is Android's own, with no background of the application's.
NavigationStack.barForegroundColor: ☑️ The actions' words take the bar's light or dark theme, as Android's own bars do; the title, the line under it, the navigation button and the pictures take the color itself - on a tab row, the chosen tab's words.
NavigationStack.toolbarStyle: – The toolbar's actions keep Android's own look.
